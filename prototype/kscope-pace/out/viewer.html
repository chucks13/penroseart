Kscope Fill — desk-wall investigation

The black-and-white Fill identity is settled. This viewer isolates what happens to definition under the exact runtime map. Fill is simulated by a flag; this prototype does not read OSC. A Fill is a Synced Mode fact served by BeatManager's Data Surface; Standalone Mode never reports one (CONTEXT.md: Fill, BeatManager, Data Surface, Standalone Mode / Synced Mode; docs/osc-client-contract.md: /rave/onair/fill_state).

The port follows Assets/effects/Kscope.cs:624-637: each mirror group's color comes from its first Tile; while Fill is active, RGB→HSV, v=(h+s+v)%1, s=0, HSV→RGB, then the group is copied. The runtime WHY is preserved: “assure there is brightness variation.”

Mono images use the real palette shape from SampleScene.unity, the GPalette.read(..., true) interpolation, the Synced PaletteSaturationFloor=0.3, and source-image brightness as in Assets/effects/Kscope.cs:600-608. The fixed human-view palette is bhw1_24_gp; the scorecard also measures every scene palette. Beat Hue is fixed at zero and the color pool's stochastic 1-in-3 channel-swap Roll is skipped so every comparison isolates Fill.
