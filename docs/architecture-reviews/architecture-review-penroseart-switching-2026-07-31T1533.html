Top recommendation

1 · Finish the doorway — then 2 · narrow construction as its natural second move

Ticket #104's destination was "every anomaly goes through one doorway" — the mechanism arrived, but the seam still leaks: a parameter that decides nothing plus two silent reads back into the asker. Making the sighting self-describing completes the effort's own stated goal, strengthens ADR-0009, and is small. Candidate 2 then pays the doorway off: once the Director answers from its arguments and its constructor names what it reads, the reflection fixtures, duplicate fakes, and 128-generation sheet hunts all fall out of the tests.
