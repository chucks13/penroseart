# The Director owns the plan; the Switcher owns the move

The Director keeps each player's current Cue Sheet and answers what to perform; the Switcher holds
the handed-over sheet in force and owns the A-to-B move — when it starts, how long it runs, and when
it lands. A Cue Mark is the beat a change *lands* on, not the beat it starts on: the start is derived
from the selected Transition's own Runway, which is why that arithmetic belongs to the Switcher.
Commands go down and questions go up — the Director pushes Standalone cadence and Show Now, the
Switcher asks what to perform at a mark, and Hold is nothing but a refused answer.
