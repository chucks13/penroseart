# A fired Cue Mark is never performed again

A Cue Sheet says what to perform at a beat, once; it is not a statement of what must be on the wall
at that beat. A mark records the beat it actually fired on and is never un-fired for the life of the
sheet, so re-crossing that beat — by loop, back-cue, or needle-drop — is one condition handled one
way: the Switcher asks the Director for an off-plan cue instead of replaying the mark. The loop lane
is not read at all.
